## Design specificity

- **Top of the page: specific to Kuuki.** The globe, the Next stub, the units translated into flying terms and the Records lead tile all belong to this product.
- **Body: generic.** The grey inset tile repeats about 15 times as card-in-card bento, which matches the "identical card grid" anti-reference.
- **Detector:** 49 hits, all false positives (padding-inline shorthand, flex-centred fixed-height controls).

## Priority issues

- **P1: one tile pattern repeated about 15 times.** Records 5, Rankings 3+3, Crossings 4. Commands: distill, layout.
- **P1: data and narrator contradictions.**
  - 9.4 shows "41h shifted" where 9.3 shows 85 h.
  - "Most trips start at 7 a.m." describes the busiest hour, not most trips.
  - Head to head counts 61 of 69 flights without saying so.
  - The 9.5 speed figure differs from 9.3.
  - Not a contradiction: "Tbilisi half of flights" and "25% of airport visits" are consistent.
  - Commands: clarify, harden.
- **P1: mobile tap targets under 44px.** + Filter, Fullscreen, Play and the month pill are 32px; the queue pill is 28; "Show" is 24; the sheet ⋯ and ✕ are 36; the sheet handle is 5. Command: adapt.
- **P1: chart marks under 3:1.** Hero year bars 1.57; map buckets 1–3 between 1.38 and 2.60; Boeing on inset 2.79; Other 1.92. Command: colorize.
- **P1: missing states, and the scope disappears on scroll.** No filtered, zero-result, first-run, n=1, loading or dark board. Commands: harden, onboard.
- **P2: section order.** Rankings first and a utility footer last. Command: layout.
- **P2: redundancy.** Busiest year ×3, Airbus vs Boeing ×3, red-eyes ×2, clock shift ×2. Command: distill.
- **P2: mobile is behind desktop.** Only the Records sheet exists; 9.5 still shows the old scatter. Command: adapt.

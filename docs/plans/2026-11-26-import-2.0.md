## Job

A traveller turns documents, Flighty history or a year of Nomads stays into journal flights, on a phone right after a trip or on a desktop going through years. Success: every candidate gets one clear decision, gaps are filled in place, and the import ends as a journal moment (flights and newly pressed stamps), not a toast.

## Why the current screen fails

A row has five independent decision mechanisms (selection checkbox, "Did you take", airport and year cards, Edit form, Include/Exclude) and the member has to infer internal state (`selected`, blocks, `review_required`). Each row gets its own question deck, so a shared gap is asked once per row. The `Ticket` signature is switched off (`mobile={false}`), the finish screen (`DoneStep`) is unmounted, and the lane shrinks to a 328×96 band at the moment it should show the reading.

## Direction

### 1. One decision per leg

Every candidate is a **leg** with exactly one visible decision: **Flew · By land · Skip** (RU «Летал · По земле · Нет»), a standard segmented control; nothing chosen means undecided.

- Flew writes the existing selection (`selected: true`, readiness rules unchanged). Skip writes `dismissed: true`. By land writes `dismissed: true` and records a land visit intent for the leg (§5).
- Confident document rows start as Flew (today's implicit selection); Nomads rows start undecided.
- No checkbox, no Include/Exclude, no "Did you take these flights?" card, no airline placeholder ("ZZ" is never shown; an unknown airline is simply absent).
- Full field editing (flight number, times, airline) stays available through a small "Edit" action that opens the existing `RowForm` in a `Sheet`.

### 2. Gaps are blanks on the leg

A missing airport or year is a dashed blank in place of the value (the city hint under it). Tapping it opens the airport picker (single endpoint, search prefilled with the hint) or the year choice. When the source offered candidates (BKK, DMK), they appear as chips under the leg. A Flew leg with an open blank cannot be saved; the blank itself is the signal (accent dashed outline), not a sentence.

### 3. Shared questions above the route

`buildQuestions` runs over all drafts once. A question that covers two or more legs (one airport set, one year group) is shown above the timeline as a compact card ("Bangkok · 2 legs: BKK / DMK"). Answering fills every blank it covers; the filled values visibly settle into the legs below. Single-leg questions stay on their leg as chips.

### 4. The year as a route

Legs are ordered by date and drawn on a vertical route:

- A **station** is a place (city name, dates of the stay when known) between two legs. Consecutive legs that share an airport or city share a station; a break in continuity starts a new segment.
- Month labels (eyebrow style) sit on the route; undated legs collect under "No date" at the end.
- The route line between stations shows the decision: solid accent for Flew, dashed muted for By land, faint for Skip, neutral for undecided. It changes as the member answers: the year assembles into a picture.
- Each leg is a compact ticket: codes in Paper Mono, city under each code, date and distance on the right, the decision control under it.
- Documents with one or two flights render the same structure with one segment; there is no special-case layout.

### 5. By land becomes passport evidence

On save, every By land leg whose destination country differs from its origin country, and whose destination country is known (airport code or a `City, CC` hint), creates a ground visit through the existing API: `countryCode` = destination country, `visitYear` = the leg's year when known, `entryMethod: 'land'`, id = the draft id (idempotent replay). Legs inside one country create nothing. The finish counts these as trips by land.

### 6. Stamps

A strip in the header shows the countries this import reaches. A country lights (pressed stamp, accent) when a Flew or By land leg reaches it; otherwise it is a ghost outline. The strip is a preview of this import's reach, not a claim about which stamps are new; the finish shows the real new stamps from the server.

### 7. The security lane is the stage

- **Hero** (no sources): larger than today on phones, full content width.
- **Stage** (any source queued or being read): the lane draws at a larger scale inside a camera box that follows the active tray, so a tray entering the tunnel and stubs leaving it are clearly visible. The rollers run while a tray travels.
- **Band** (review): a low strip once every source is read.
- **Done**: the tray leaves the lane on the finish.
- Contract unchanged: nothing moves on first render; reduced motion places trays at once and keeps the monitor lit.

### 8. Finish on the page

Saving keeps the member on `/import` and turns the screen into the finish: the saved count as a `SplitFlap`, "+N countries" with `StampSection` (new stamps from the server, pressed one by one), the land trips count, and actions to open the journal or the passport. The shell success notice still fires. Failed or uncertain legs stay listed with their retry, as today.

### 9. Layout

- **Phone (≤700px):** header (title, close, stamp strip), lane, source lines (file name and its one-line summary; "Left out" behind a disclosure), shared questions, route. A floating bar holds the decided count and the primary action "Add N to journal".
- **Desktop:** two columns. Left, sticky: lane, sources, stamp strip, primary action. Right: shared questions and the route.
- The floating bar never covers the focused control (scroll padding at every width).

### 10. Copy

Every new string in EN and RU, real plural forms, «вы». After the build, a `stop-describing-ui` pass rewrites every string in the flow (before/after table): action labels with specific verbs, no sentences that describe the feature, storage or what the screen already shows.

## Boundaries

- No server change except what §5 needs from existing endpoints; draft, admission and confirm contracts stay as they are.
- Flighty history (`HistoryImport`) keeps its own flow in this round.
- Visual world unchanged: tokens, Commissioner and Paper Mono, iso figures, radii. No new palette.
- Anti-goals: a CRM-style list of bordered form cards, explanatory paragraphs, more than one primary action on screen, motion on first render.

## States and ranges

1 to 500 legs per source, several sources at once; dated, partly dated (month-day, ordinal) and undated legs; known codes, airport options, city hints without airports; duplicates of journal flights; save partial failure and uncertain outcomes; reload mid-review (session retention); empty export; reduced motion; EN/RU; light and dark; 320px to wide desktop.

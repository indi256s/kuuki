# White Sheet migration

Move `apps/next` onto the White Sheet design system. The specification is the versioned catalog in [`design-system/`](../../design-system/README.md) (`palette.json`, contracts, `validate.mjs`). The application stays the canonical token owner: `apps/next/src/app/styles.css`. Branch: `feat/white-sheet-migration`. Execution status belongs in an adjacent `2026-10-15-white-sheet-migration-handoff.md` once work spans sessions.

## Accepted inputs

- Catalog copied and versioned (`294efcf1`); pre-migration role revisions committed (`8268c694`): default accent without `data-accent`, `mark-peak = accent-text`, ramps and slots CIEDE2000 ≥ 8 from it, status text ≥ 10 from `accent-text` with a mandatory icon or label, danger/info/hover values, state/surface/figure/motion contracts.
- Owner decisions (15 October 2026): white light page, Shu default with dark foreground, dark White Sheet surfaces (card darker than page, floating lighter); neutral marks plus a coloured selection; fixed categories; comparison slots keep dash and marker; heat carries a numeric legend; known zero and missing stay distinct. Ai, Matcha, Asagi and Sakura keep inherited fills; no new accent names, storage protocol or picker changes. No glow on UI, no glass, no decorative gradients. Category-coloured charts never use `mark-peak`.

## Decisions for the migration

1. **Shared roles equal the spec.** For the 71 catalog roles, each resolved mode × accent set in `styles.css` equals `palette.json`. A test in `tokens.test.ts` reads `design-system/palette.json` and fails on drift; a deliberate change updates the spec, runs its `check`, then the app.
2. **App-only roles stay app-owned.** Makers beyond the catalog six, regions, alliances, gate, holder, pass, stamp inks and iso shading keep their values. Where new surfaces push one under its existing contrast test, the smallest lightness shift that passes is used and listed in the wave report for owner review.
3. **Relations replace aliases.** `--mark-rest` stops aliasing `--heat-3`; `--mark-peak` stops aliasing `--mark-1`/`--heat-4`. `--accent-hover` becomes a literal. `--mark-1…4`, `--kc-chart-*` and `--map-bucket-*` keep their aliases.
4. **Mirror follows the CSS.** `packages/shared/src/theme/palette.ts` gains `markRest` and `markPeak` per mode × accent so non-CSS consumers stop inferring them from heat or comparison indexes. `THEME_COLOR`, `SURFACE_COLOR`, `NEUTRAL_INK` and the favicon hairline follow the new surfaces.
5. **States.** `outline` is focus only. Selected, pressed and linked states draw an inset `box-shadow` or border in `--selection-contour`; forced colors use a `Highlight` border.
6. **Surfaces.** A surface nested in a surface keeps a `--hairline` border in both themes; top-level dark in-flow cards keep their current transparent border and top highlight.
7. **Figures.** Iso figures read their live paint from `--figure-live-fill` (default the accent fill). A figure part that depicts a category sets it to that category's token.

## Out of scope

Push, PR, deployment, remote D1 and landing on `main` need separate authorisation. No new accents, storage keys, picker changes or API changes. Concorde and passport generated art (`*.generated.js`) keep their palettes unless a wave report shows a contrast failure on the new surfaces.

## Risks

- Light Shu flips its accent foreground from white to `#202020`; every filled-accent label must read `--accent-foreground`, not a literal white.
- The white page lowers contrast of mid-tone app-only colours (makers, regions, airline brands); decision 2 handles failures one by one.
- Navigation tests share controls across journeys; any UI wave runs the full Playwright suite serially.

# Ground travel is organized around journeys

Rail journeys group individual legs chosen by the traveler, while a road trip
contains its whole ordered itinerary, including any return travel. Treating
every car segment as a separate record or showing train legs as the primary
experience would obscure the journey the traveler wants to plan and remember.
This grouping does not change flight connection inference. Cross-mode grouping,
storage and routing are not decided here.

Actual land entry into a country, including transit without a stop, establishes
a country visit after the traveler confirms completion and the crossed
countries. Planned routes and approximate map lines do not establish visits;
flight transit rules remain unchanged.

Completed journeys retain the actual itinerary rather than untraveled planned
segments. Editing or removing a journey removes only its own country evidence;
other evidence can continue to support the same passport stamp.
